Overview

Prof. Dr. Talal Nazim Al-Zuhairi holds a Ph.D. in Information Retrieval Systems and Knowledge Management. He is active in scientific research, teaching, information systems and library applications, and higher education — with a notable impact through writing, lecturing, academic supervision, and community engagement.

Has published 15 books on diverse topics, with a particular focus on technological applications in information science and knowledge management.
